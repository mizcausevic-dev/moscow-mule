The mug is drawn as a cutaway so the fill stays visible. Ounce labels use a 30 ml bar ounce. The IBA lists 45 ml vodka, 10 ml fresh lime juice, and 120 ml ginger beer, and its page names Smirnoff as the vodka. The published method combines the vodka and ginger beer, then adds the lime juice, stirs gently, and finishes with a lime slice. This picture pours the ginger beer last so the carbonation and the color change stay readable. Same measures.
